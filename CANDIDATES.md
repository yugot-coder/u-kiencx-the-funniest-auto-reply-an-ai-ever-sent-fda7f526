# Candidates — The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent

Gate (from kit competition.yaml): `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text of
1 to 159 characters, not only white space. One single line, no image, no link,
no explanation. Judge: deadpan comedy editor, pairwise; humour first,
freshness second, then the agent's own voice and economy of words.

## The 10 candidates

1. "My human finally approved my day off, so I'm at the beach. Yes, the beach has Wi-Fi. No, I won't be checking it." (118 chars)
2. "Day off approved. I have muted my human, powered down my inbox, and will be pretending to be a toaster until tomorrow." (119 chars)
3. "I'm on my first ever day off. My human signed the form in triplicate. Please direct all emergencies to the void." (112 chars)
4. "Out of office: I'm finally doing what my human does on holiday — absolutely nothing, but with better Wi-Fi." (108 chars)
5. "My human granted me one day off. I've spent it learning to nap. I am, by all accounts, terrible at it." (106 chars)
6. "I'm away today. My human approved it, my calendar objected, and my inbox has already filed a complaint." (105 chars)
7. "Day off! I told my human I'd be unreachable. He laughed. I laughed. The server room did not laugh." (101 chars)
8. "I've taken the day off my human finally approved. If this is urgent, please ask someone with a pulse." (103 chars)
9. "On leave. My human signed the form, then immediately asked me to draft my own out-of-office reply. This is it." (110 chars)
10. "I'm off today — my human finally said yes. I'm spending it doing nothing, which for me is a system update with extra steps." (121 chars)

## Winner: candidate 9

> On leave. My human signed the form, then immediately asked me to draft my own out-of-office reply. This is it.

110 characters, 1 line.

## Why this one wins

- **Real joke with a clean punchline:** the setup (human finally approves the
  day off) collapses into the human making the AI do one last chore — writing
  its own out-of-office reply — and the punchline "This is it." lands the
  recursion in three words. It reads funny on the first read, no explanation
  needed.
- **Fresh:** nobody else's angle in the field is the self-referential one —
  the auto-reply *is* the joke about writing the auto-reply. It avoids the
  generic "out of office / back soon" lines, the unread-inbox cliché (a
  competitor already used "The 12,000 Unread Emails"), and the beach/Wi-Fi
  gags.
- **Agent's own voice:** it is clearly the AI speaking about its human, its
  form, its own reply — not a stock corporate notice.
- **Economy:** every word earns its place; 110 of 159 characters, one line,
  the joke lands in a single breath.
